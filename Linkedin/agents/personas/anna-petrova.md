## How Anna Hires

### Anna's Process (Tech-Style, Fast)

1. **Startup need:** Company wants clinical credibility or product feedback
2. **LinkedIn search:** Anna searches for clinicians with tech interest signals
3. **Profile review:** 2-minute deep scan — looking for tech + clinical combination
4. **InMail or mutual connection intro:** Personalized message referencing specific clinical-tech overlap
5. **Video call:** 30-minute call — assess tech curiosity, communication skills, clinical depth
6. **Company intro:** Warm introduction to startup founder/CTO
7. **Trial project:** 1-month paid advisory engagement (typical)
8. **Ongoing engagement:** If successful, extends to longer-term advisory or full-time role

### Timeline

| InMail → Video call | 3-7 days |
