## How David Hires

### David's Hiring Process (Informal, Fast)

Unlike Kupat Holim's structured process, David hires personally:

1. **Need identified:** Too many endo referrals leaving the clinic (revenue loss)
2. **Network first:** Asks colleagues, checks LinkedIn, word-of-mouth
3. **LinkedIn browse:** Searches for endodontists, reads profiles, checks posts
4. **Coffee meeting:** Informal 45-minute meeting at a café near the clinic
5. **Trial day:** 1-2 days seeing real patients (compensated)
6. **Decision:** David decides personally within 48 hours
7. **Arrangement:** Revenue-sharing agreement or per-procedure rate

### Timeline

| Stage | Duration |
|---|---|
| Need → First contact | 1-2 weeks (ongoing search) |
| Coffee meeting → Trial day | 3-5 days |
| Trial day → Agreement | 2-3 days |
| **Total** | **2-3 weeks** |

## Interview Expectations (Coffee Meeting)

### David's Questions (Informal, Conversational)

1. "אז ספר לי על עצמך — מאיפה אתה, מה הסיפור?" (So tell me about yourself — where are you from, what's the story?)
2. "כמה טיפולי שורש אתה עושה בחודש ממוצע?" (How many root canals do you do in an average month?)
3. "יש לך ניסיון עם מיקרוסקופ? מאיזה יצרן? כמה שעות?" (Do you have microscope experience? Which manufacturer? How many hours?)
4. "איך אתה מתמודד עם חולה מפוחד שלא רוצה טיפול שורש?" (How do you handle a scared patient who doesn't want a root canal?)
5. "מה אתה מחפש מבחינת סידור עבודה?" (What are you looking for in terms of work arrangement?)
6. "יש לך חולים שילכו אחריך?" (Do you have patients who would follow you?)
7. "מה דעתך על שיתוף הכנסות לעומת תעריף קבוע?" (What do you think about revenue sharing vs. fixed rate?)

### Trial Day Assessment (What David Watches)

| Observation | What David Is Really Evaluating |
|---|---|
| Patient greeting | "Does the patient feel comfortable immediately?" |
| Treatment explanation | "Does he explain at the right level? Not too technical, not too simple?" |
| Microscope setup | "How fast and confident? Has he really used it 375+ hours?" |
| Interaction with staff | "Will my dental assistants enjoy working with him?" |
| Patient farewell | "Would this patient recommend us to friends?" |
| Between patients | "Is he friendly with my team? Will he fit our culture?" |

## How David Evaluates LinkedIn Profiles

### What Catches David's Eye

1. **Photos/visual content:** Clinical case posts with before/after descriptions → "This person documents quality work"
2. **Microscope content:** Posts about surgical microscope → "They're genuinely passionate about precision endo"
3. **Patient interaction stories:** Posts about patient communication → "They understand the private practice experience"
4. **Professional photo:** Clean, approachable → "My patients would trust this person"
5. **Activity level:** Regular posting → "They're building a professional brand — that helps my clinic too"

### David's LinkedIn Search

David doesn't use LinkedIn Recruiter. He searches organically:
```
Search: "endodontist microscope Israel"
Browse: check posts, about section, recommendations
Decision: "Do I want to meet this person for coffee?"
```

He's more influenced by **content quality** than keyword optimization.
