## Hiring Process (Kupat Holim)

### How Yael Hires

1. **Position opens:** Clinic director submits staffing request with specialty requirements
2. **Job posted:** Internal board (Clalit intranet) + LinkedIn + Israeli dental job boards
3. **Initial screening:** Yael reviews CVs — 30-second scan per CV, 2-3 minutes for promising candidates
4. **LinkedIn check:** Searches LinkedIn for candidates matching specialty + location
5. **Phone screen:** 15-minute Hebrew phone call — availability, salary expectations, license status
6. **Committee interview:** Panel of clinic director + medical director + HR representative
7. **Trial day:** 1-day clinical observation (mandatory for specialists)
8. **Reference check:** 2-3 references, always including most recent employer
9. **Offer:** Standardized salary band + benefits package (non-negotiable on base, flexible on hours)

### Timeline

| Stage | Duration |
|---|---|
| CV review → Phone screen | 3-5 business days |
| Phone screen → Committee | 1-2 weeks |
| Committee → Trial day | 1 week |
| Trial day → Offer | 3-5 business days |
| **Total** | **4-6 weeks** (faster for urgent needs) |

## Interview Expectations (Yael's Panel)

### Phone Screen Questions (Yael asks)

1. "ספר/י לי על הניסיון הקליני שלך — כמה שנים, אילו טיפולים" (Tell me about your clinical experience — years, procedures)
2. "מה הזמינות שלך? מתי את/ה יכול/ה להתחיל?" (What's your availability? When can you start?)
3. "מה ציפיות השכר שלך?" (What are your salary expectations?)
4. "יש לך רישיון ישראלי?" (Do you have an Israeli license?)
5. "את/ה מוכן/ה לעבוד בכמה סניפים?" (Are you willing to work across multiple branches?)

### Committee Interview Questions

1. Clinical scenario: "חולה מגיע עם כאב חריף, הצילום מראה שבר בכלי — מה הגישה שלך?" (Patient arrives with acute pain, X-ray shows instrument separation — what's your approach?)
2. Teamwork: "איך את/ה עובד/ת עם עוזרי שיניים ורופאים מפנים?" (How do you work with dental assistants and referring dentists?)
3. Volume: "בכללית אנחנו עובדים בקצב גבוה — מה הניסיון שלך בסביבה דומה?" (At Clalit we work at high volume — what's your experience in similar environments?)
4. IDF follow-up: "איך הניסיון הצבאי שלך מתרגם לעבודה בקופה?" (How does your military experience translate to HMO work?)
5. Language: "יש לך חולים דוברי רוסית — איך את/ה מתמודד/ת?" (You have Russian-speaking patients — how do you handle this?)

### Trial Day Assessment

Yael's clinic director evaluates:
- Clinical competency (observed procedures)
- Patient communication (Hebrew + Russian if applicable)
- Team interaction (works well with staff?)
- Pace (can handle Clalit patient volume?)
- Equipment familiarity (microscope, CBCT if available)
