## Israeli Dental Market Structure

| Segment | Details | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Private clinics** | ~85% of dentists work in private practice or group practice | PayScale IL 2025, NBN Dentistry page |
| **Kupot Holim clinics** | Clalit, Maccabi, Meuhedet, Leumit operate for-profit dental clinics; adult dental NOT in basic health basket | NBN Dental Care page |
| **Salary — general dentist** | ₪380,000–434,000/year (~₪183–209/hr) | PayScale IL 2025, ERI Economic Research 2025, SalaryExpert IL |
| **Salary — oral surgeon** | ₪633,000/year (~₪304/hr) — specialist premium ~50-65% over general | ERI Economic Research IL 2025 |
| **Salary — public sector** | ₪351,790/year — notably lower than private practice average | SalaryExpert IL (Public Health Dentist) |
| **Specialist model** | Specialists commonly work in multiple clinics + HMOs simultaneously | PMC: Strategic changes of dental clinics in Israel (2024) |
| **Clinic structure** | Multidisciplinary practices, not solely referral-based; competitive market | PMC: Strategic changes of dental clinics in Israel (2024) |
| **Payment model (Kupot)** | Dentists paid per procedure; restoration on child ≈ 30-40 NIS | Times of Israel: Working Conditions (public sector) |
| **Turnover** | High turnover in public sector — young graduates leave after few years | Times of Israel: Working Conditions |
| **Large chains** | Employ dentists at scale, offer dentistry at lower cost via large patient pools | Raanana Dental Care guide |

## Licensing for Foreign-Trained Dentists

| Requirement | Details | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Standard path** | Pass government licensing examinations (theoretical + practical), held twice/year | Ministry of Health: Dentistry Licensure |
| **Exemption path** | 5 years practice in last 7 years at accredited institution → exempt from exam (since 2016 Knesset amendment) | Law Offices Michael Decker; Library of Congress 2016 |
| **Degree recognition** | Ministry of Health recognizes foreign dental programs ≥5 years (excl. internship); no online degrees | Ministry of Health: Accreditation page |
| **Nostrification** | Starting Jan 2025, expedited process available | Gov.il: Licensing recognition |
| **IDF dentists (under 34)** | Must complete ≥2 years conscription; army may cap exemptions if high demand | NBN: Army Requirements for Dentists |
| **Cost reimbursement** | Ministry of Aliyah offers retroactive ≤4,000 NIS for document translation/notarization | NBN: Licensing Process |

## IDF Service Value in Hiring

| Signal | Impact | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **IDF service on resume** | Highly regarded — shows responsibility, dedication, teamwork | EasyAliyah: Israeli Resume Guide; ResumeFlex: Israel CV Tips |
| **Military section placement** | Listed after education, include corps + rank | NBN: Israeli Resume Format; EasyAliyah |
| **Elite units (8200, Talpiot)** | "Resume gold" — alumni network opens doors, viewed as equivalent to top education | ResumeFlex: Israel CV Tips |
| **Officer rank (Captain/Seren)** | Requires 4+ years of promotions; signals sustained commitment + leadership | Wikipedia: IDF ranks |
| **Medical Corps specificity** | Dental clinic commander = leadership + clinical credibility combined | IDF Medical Corps: Wikipedia; JMVH publication |
| **Skills transfer** | Triage, resource optimization, team leadership, pressure decision-making | ResumeFlex: Israel Job Interview Guide |
| **Career military (Keva)** | After demobilization, employers actively offer specialist positions | ResumeFlex: Israel CV Guide |
| **No IDF service (olim)** | Acceptable to omit section; compensate with other Israeli signals | EasyAliyah: Israeli Resume |
