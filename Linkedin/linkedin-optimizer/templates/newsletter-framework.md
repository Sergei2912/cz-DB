# LinkedIn Newsletter Framework

LinkedIn Newsletters deliver directly to subscribers' inboxes and notifications. Monthly publication builds thought leadership and generates consistent engagement.

> Source: Content pillars from `profile-context.yaml`. Vocabulary from Medical Lexicon DB.

## Why Newsletter

- Subscribers receive email + push notification (higher reach than regular posts)
- Newsletters are SEO-indexed by Google (long-term discoverability)
- Author badge on profile signals thought leadership
- Monthly rhythm matches existing content calendar without overcommitting
- Maya Cohen's insight: "A dental professional with an active newsletter stands out immediately — it signals they're a thought leader, not just a job seeker."

## Newsletter Structure (6 Sections)

### 1. Personal Opening (150-200 words)

Start with a recent experience, observation, or question that sets the theme.

**Tone:** Personal, reflective, draws reader in. Not a formal introduction.

**Examples:**
- "Last week, a referring dentist told me the tooth was hopeless. Here's what I found under the microscope..."
- "It's been exactly one year since I returned to Israel from Prague. Three things surprised me..."
- "A patient asked me in Russian why Israeli dentists don't use microscopes. It made me think..."

### 2. Main Article (600-900 words)

Deep-dive into the monthly topic. This is the core value of the newsletter.

**Structure:**
- Problem or question statement
- Context with data (from Universita Hub — never hardcoded)
- Personal experience or clinical case
- Analysis or insight
- Practical takeaway

**Content rules:**
- Include 3-5 Medical Lexicon terms (Clinical Verbs, Dental Terms)
- At least 2 quantified metrics from Notion data
- Reference relevant research or industry trends
- Write at a level accessible to general dental professionals (not just endodontists)

### 3. Clinical Tip of the Month (150-200 words)

One actionable clinical insight — practical, specific, immediately useful.

**Format:**
```
💡 Clinical Tip: [Title]

[1-2 sentence context]
[The specific technique or approach]
[Expected outcome or benefit]
[One-line caveat or when NOT to use this]
```

**Source:** Rotate across pillars — microscope technique, CBCT interpretation, patient communication, instrument selection.

### 4. Israeli Market Insight (150-200 words)

Market intelligence relevant to dental professionals in Israel. Draws from Maya Cohen's market knowledge.

**Topics rotation:**
- Hiring trends (which specialties are in demand)
- Regulatory updates (licensing, Kupot Holim policies)
- Technology adoption rates in Israeli clinics
- Salary benchmarks or career path observations
- Dental startup ecosystem updates

### 5. Resource Recommendation (100-150 words)

One curated resource — article, tool, course, book, or event.

**Format:**
```
📚 This Month's Pick: [Resource Name]
Type: [Article / Course / Tool / Book / Event]
Why: [1-2 sentences on why it's valuable]
Link: [URL if available]
```

**Rules:**
- Alternate between free and paid resources
- Include at least 1 Hebrew-language resource every quarter
- Never recommend direct competitors' services without objective framing

### 6. Reader Engagement (50-100 words)

End with an interactive element to drive comments and shares.

**Options (rotate monthly):**
- Question to readers: "What's your biggest challenge with [topic]?"
- Poll suggestion: "How often do you use [technology] in your practice?"
- Case submission: "Share your most challenging case — I'll feature one next month"
- Feedback request: "What topic should I cover next?"

## Design Guidelines

- **Header image:** Consistent branded banner with newsletter name + issue number
- **Formatting:** Use headers (H2, H3), bold for key terms, bullet points for lists
- **Readability:** Short paragraphs (3-4 sentences max), white space between sections
- **Bilingual elements:** Hebrew terms in parentheses after English, Hebrew section headers where appropriate
- **Mobile-first:** Most LinkedIn users read on mobile — test readability on narrow screens

## Content Bank Integration

When saving newsletter content:
- **Type:** "Post" (LinkedIn categorizes newsletters as a post subtype)
- **Pillar:** Matching pillar name for the main article topic
- **Notes:** "Format: Newsletter — Issue #X, [month year]"
- **Language:** "English" or "EN+HE" if bilingual sections included
- **Status:** "Draft" until published

## Launch Checklist

Before publishing the first newsletter issue:
- [ ] Newsletter name and description set on LinkedIn
- [ ] Branded header image created
- [ ] First 3 issues drafted (buffer for consistent publishing)
- [ ] Posting schedule confirmed (1st Thursday of month)
- [ ] Promotion plan: announce newsletter launch in a regular post
