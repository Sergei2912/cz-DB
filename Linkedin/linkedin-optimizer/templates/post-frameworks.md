#### Framework
```

[1-2 sentences setting the clinical scenario — anonymized]

THE CHALLENGE:
[Describe what made this case complex]

MY APPROACH:
[Step-by-step methodology, tools used]
• [Step 1 — e.g., CBCT analysis revealed...]
• [Step 2 — e.g., Under surgical microscope...]
• [Step 3 — e.g., NiTi rotary instrumentation...]

THE RESULT:
[Outcome + what was learned]

KEY TAKEAWAY:
[One actionable insight for other dentists]

What's the most challenging endo case you've encountered?
👇 Share in the comments

#Endodontics #DentalMicroscope #RootCanal #ClinicalCase
#IsraeliDentistry #אנדודונטיה
```

## Post Checklist
- [ ] Hook in first 2 lines (visible before "see more")
- [ ] Personal story or data (not generic advice)
- [ ] IDF/military reference when relevant
- [ ] Call-to-action question at the end
- [ ] 3-5 relevant hashtags (mix EN + HE)
- [ ] Tag relevant people/companies when appropriate
- [ ] Post on Sunday or Thursday (Israeli business days)
