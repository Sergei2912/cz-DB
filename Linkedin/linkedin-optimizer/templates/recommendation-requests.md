# Recommendation Request Templates

Templates for requesting LinkedIn recommendations from professional contacts. **User sends manually** — agent generates personalized drafts.

> Cross-reference: Outreach pipeline in `agents/outreach-agent.md`. Endorsement strategy in `endorsement-strategy.md`.

## Why Recommendations Matter

- Profiles with 3+ recommendations rank higher in recruiter searches
- Recommendations provide third-party validation that can't be self-claimed
- Maya Cohen's insight: "I always check recommendations — they tell me what the candidate's colleagues actually think, not just what the candidate says about themselves"
- Target: 5-8 recommendations covering different relationship types

## Request Principles

1. **Make it easy** — suggest specific topics they could address
2. **Be specific** — mention the project/period you'd like them to cover
3. **Offer reciprocity** — offer to write one for them first
4. **Time it right** — request after a positive interaction, not out of the blue
5. **Follow up once** — if no response after 7 days, send a gentle reminder

## 1. IDF Commanding Officer / Military Colleague

**Best for:** Leadership, team management, pressure decision-making

**Language:** Hebrew

```
[שם], שלום!

אני מעדכן את פרופיל הלינקדאין שלי ומחפש המלצות מאנשים שעבדתי איתם בצה"ל.
עבדנו יחד ב[יחידה/תקופה] ואני מאוד מעריך את [ניסיון ספציפי יחד].

אם יש לך כמה דקות, אשמח מאוד להמלצה קצרה שמתייחסת ל:
- [מיומנות ספציפית שראו — למשל: ניהול צוות רפואי תחת לחץ]
- [תוצאה ספציפית — למשל: הקמת מערך טיפולי שיניים ביחידה]

כמובן שאשמח לכתוב המלצה גם עבורך.
תודה רבה! 🙏
```

**Suggested topics for recommender:**
- Leadership under pressure
- Clinical decision-making in field conditions
- Team building and training
- Resource optimization
- Protocol development

## 2. Clinic Owner / Employer

**Best for:** Clinical skills, patient care, technical proficiency

**Language:** English (adapt to Hebrew for Israeli employers)

```
Dear [Name],

I'm updating my LinkedIn profile and would greatly appreciate a recommendation from you, given our professional relationship at [clinic name].

During my time at [clinic/period], I particularly valued [specific positive experience — e.g., "the opportunity to handle complex retreatment cases using the surgical microscope"].

If you have a few minutes, a brief recommendation touching on any of these areas would be incredibly helpful:
- Clinical proficiency in endodontics (especially microscope-assisted procedures)
- Patient management and communication
- [Any specific project or achievement during your time there]

I'd be happy to write a recommendation for you as well. Thank you for considering this!

Best regards,
Sergii
```

**Suggested topics for recommender:**
- Clinical precision and outcomes
- Patient satisfaction and communication
- Adoption of advanced technology (microscope, CBCT)
- Reliability, professionalism
- Multilingual patient care

## 3. University Professor / Academic Mentor

**Best for:** Academic foundation, research aptitude, clinical training

**Language:** English

```
Dear Professor [Name],

I hope you're well. I'm updating my professional LinkedIn profile and would be honored to have a recommendation from you, given your role in my education at [Charles University / institution].

Your [course/supervision/mentoring] in [specific area] was foundational to my development as an endodontist. I still apply [specific technique or principle] in my daily practice.

Would you consider writing a brief recommendation addressing:
- Academic performance and clinical aptitude during [program/period]
- [Specific skill or project you excelled in]
- Research or analytical capabilities

I would be grateful for any mention of these areas. Of course, I'm happy to provide any details that would make this easier for you.

With appreciation,
Sergii Anipreyev, DMD
Charles University, [graduation year]
```

**Suggested topics for recommender:**
- Academic excellence
- Clinical skill development
- Research methodology
- Professional dedication
- International perspective

## 4. Professional Colleague / Fellow Specialist

**Best for:** Peer validation, clinical collaboration, professional respect

**Language:** English or Hebrew (match their preference)

**English version:**
```
Hi [Name],

I've always valued our professional interactions, especially [specific collaboration — e.g., "our discussions about complex endodontic cases" or "the case you referred to me with the calcified canals"].

I'm building out my LinkedIn presence and would appreciate a peer recommendation from you. Even a brief note about our professional interaction would be meaningful.

Areas you might touch on:
- Clinical approach and case management
- Collaboration and communication
- Technical skills (microscope, CBCT interpretation)

Happy to reciprocate — I'd be glad to write one for you as well.

Best,
Sergii
```

**Hebrew version:**
```
היי [שם],

אני תמיד מעריך את הקשר המקצועי שלנו, במיוחד [שיתוף פעולה ספציפי].

אני מפתח את הנוכחות שלי בלינקדאין ואשמח להמלצה מקצועית ממך. גם כמה שורות קצרות יהיו בעלות ערך רב.

נושאים אפשריים:
- גישה קלינית וניהול מקרים
- שיתוף פעולה ותקשורת מקצועית
- מיומנויות טכניות (מיקרוסקופ, CBCT)

כמובן שאשמח לכתוב המלצה גם עבורך.

בברכה,
סרגיי
```

## Timing Strategy

| Relationship Type | Best Time to Request |
|---|---|
| IDF colleague | After reconnecting via LinkedIn (Day 7-14 of outreach sequence) |
| Clinic owner | After completing a successful case or before departing a position |
| Professor | Academic milestone (graduation anniversary, publication) |
| Peer colleague | After a successful collaboration or case referral |
| MedTech contact | After providing clinical feedback or beta testing |

## Follow-up (If No Response After 7 Days)

```
Hi [Name], just a gentle follow-up on my recommendation request. I completely understand if you're busy — even 2-3 sentences would be incredibly helpful. No pressure at all, and thank you for considering it!
```

## Quality Markers (What Maya Cohen Looks For)

Maya evaluates recommendations for:
- **Specificity:** Does it mention specific skills/outcomes, or is it generic?
- **Credibility:** Is the recommender someone whose opinion carries weight?
- **Recency:** Recommendations from the last 2-3 years carry more weight
- **Relevance:** Does it align with the target role (endodontics, Israeli market)?
- **Diversity:** Mix of supervisors, peers, and subordinates shows well-rounded professional relationships
