## General Tips for Israeli Interviews
- Be direct and confident (Israeli culture values это)
- Use numbers and data whenever possible
- Don't be overly formal — conversational tone is expected
- Ask questions back — shows engagement
- Reference IDF experience early — it's universally respected
- Prepare 2-3 questions about the clinic/company
- Salary negotiation is expected — use LinkedIn Premium data
