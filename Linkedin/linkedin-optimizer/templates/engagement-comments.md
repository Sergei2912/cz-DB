# Engagement Comments Framework

5 comment types with templates for strategic LinkedIn engagement. Comments build visibility, relationships, and thought leadership.

## Rules

- **Max 300 characters** for initial visibility (longer comments get truncated)
- Comment within **2 hours** of post publication for maximum algorithm boost
- **Never generic** ("Great post!", "Thanks for sharing") — always add value
- Language: match the post language (HE for Hebrew posts, EN for English)
- Include 1 Medical Lexicon term when commenting on clinical content
- Target: 10 comments/week across priority contacts and industry posts

---

## Comment Type 1: Expert Insight

**When to use:** Posts about clinical topics, dental technology, or healthcare trends where you can add professional perspective.

**Structure:** Validate + Add unique angle + Question

### Template by Relationship Type

**Colleague / Peer:**
```
Great observation about [topic]. In my experience with [X]+ [procedures], I've found that [unique insight from your practice]. Have you tried [specific approach]?
```

**Senior / KOL:**
```
This resonates strongly. During my [X] years focusing on [specialty], [supporting data point from your experience]. Your point about [specific aspect] is particularly relevant for [Israeli context].
```

**Industry / MedTech:**
```
Interesting perspective on [product/technology]. From the clinical side, what we need most is [specific need]. [Quantified experience with similar technology]. Would love to see [specific feature/improvement].
```

## Comment Type 2: Thoughtful Question

**When to use:** Posts where asking a smart question shows expertise and sparks discussion.

**Structure:** Context + Specific question

### Templates

**Clinical posts:**
```
Fascinating case. Curious about your approach to [specific clinical aspect] — did you consider [alternative technique]? I've seen different outcomes with [your experience].
```

**Career / Industry posts:**
```
Important topic. How do you see this affecting [specific Israeli market segment]? In my transition from [IDF/Europe], I noticed [relevant observation].
```

**Technology posts:**
```
Great overview. Have you compared [Technology A] vs [Technology B] for [specific use case]? My [X]+ hours with [technology] suggest [specific insight].
```

## Comment Type 3: Data Share

**When to use:** Posts making claims without data, or where your numbers add credibility.

**Structure:** Acknowledge claim + Share your data + Contextualize

### Templates

**Supporting data:**
```
Spot on. My data from [X]+ procedures confirms this — [specific metric from Universita Hub]. In the Israeli market specifically, [contextual observation].
```

**Contrasting data:**
```
Interesting finding. My experience in [context] shows slightly different numbers: [your metric]. Could be [explanation for difference]. Would love to compare methodologies.
```

**Adding Israeli context:**
```
This is especially relevant in Israel where [specific market condition]. From my work with [patient population], [data point]. The [Kupot Holim / private sector] dynamic adds another layer.
```

## Comment Type 4: Personal Story

**When to use:** Posts about career transitions, challenges, cultural topics, or personal growth where vulnerability builds connection.

**Structure:** "This reminds me of..." + Brief personal story + Connection to their point

### Templates

**IDF-related:**
```
This hits close to home. During my 8 years in the IDF Medical Corps, [brief relevant experience]. Your point about [aspect] is exactly what I tell fellow veterans transitioning to civilian practice.
```

**Immigration / Cultural:**
```
As someone who moved from Czech Republic to Israel, I completely relate. The moment I realized [specific realization] changed my approach to [professional aspect]. [Brief lesson learned].
```

**Clinical growth:**
```
I remember my first [specific case type] — [brief honest moment]. [X] years and [Y]+ procedures later, [how perspective changed]. Your post captures that growth mindset perfectly.
```

---

## Comment Type 5: Resource Share

**When to use:** Posts where you can add value by sharing a relevant resource, article, tool, or connection.

**Structure:** Add to conversation + Specific resource + Why it's relevant

### Templates

**Article / Study:**
```
Building on this — [specific study/guideline from AAE/ADA] found [relevant finding]. Especially useful for practitioners considering [topic]. Happy to share the reference if anyone's interested.
```

**Tool / Technology:**
```
For anyone implementing this, [specific tool/technique] has been a game-changer in my practice. [Brief result]. Took about [timeframe] to integrate but worth it.
```

**Connection / Event:**
```
Great discussion. [Person/organization] is doing interesting work on this in Israel. Also, [event/conference] covers this topic annually — worth checking out for those interested.
```

## Engagement Cadence

| Day | Target | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Sunday | 3 comments | Priority contacts + industry posts |
| Monday | 2 comments | Israeli dental community |
| Tuesday | 2 comments | IDF / veteran network |
| Wednesday | 2 comments | MedTech / innovation posts |
| Thursday | 1-2 comments | Engage with responses to your own post |

**Priority order for commenting:**
1. Posts from contacts in your Outreach Pipeline (relationship building)
2. Posts from target companies / hiring managers (visibility)
3. Posts from KOLs in Israeli dental (association by proximity)
4. Popular posts in your groups (broad reach)
5. Posts using your target keywords (SEO adjacency)

## Quality Checklist

- [ ] Adds genuine value (not just agreement)
- [ ] Under 300 characters for visibility
- [ ] Includes specific data point or personal experience
- [ ] Matches post language (EN or HE)
- [ ] No self-promotion in first sentence
- [ ] Ends with question or invitation to continue discussion
- [ ] Uses Medical Lexicon term when clinically relevant
