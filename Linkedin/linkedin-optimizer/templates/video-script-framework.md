# Video Script Framework

5 video script templates by content pillar. All follow the 4-act 60-second format optimized for LinkedIn native video.

## Universal Structure (60 seconds = 150-180 words)

| Timestamp | Act | Duration | Words | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:05 | **Hook** | 5 sec | 15-20 | Stop the scroll — question or surprising stat |
| 0:05-0:20 | **Context** | 15 sec | 40-50 | Personal story or clinical case setup |
| 0:20-0:45 | **Insight** | 25 sec | 60-70 | 1 key point with supporting data |
| 0:45-0:55 | **Takeaway** | 10 sec | 25-30 | Actionable advice in 1-2 sentences |
| 0:55-1:00 | **CTA** | 5 sec | 15-20 | Follow, comment, share experience |

## Visual Cue Markers

Use these inline markers in scripts:
- `[VISUAL: description]` — B-roll or visual element
- `[SUBTITLE: text]` — Hebrew subtitle suggestion
- `[TEXT ON SCREEN: text]` — Text overlay
- `[THUMBNAIL: text]` — Scroll-stopper frame text

## Production Notes

**Filming tips for all templates:**
- Film in landscape (16:9) for LinkedIn native video
- Good lighting on face — natural light or ring light
- Clean, professional background (clinic or bookshelf)
- Lapel mic or phone close to mouth — audio quality > video quality
- Record in 1 take if possible — authenticity over polish
- Add captions (LinkedIn auto-captions are 80% accurate — review them)

**Content Bank:** Save with Type: "Post", Notes: "Format: Video Script — 60s, Pillar: [pillar]"
