# LinkedIn Carousel (Document Post) Templates

LinkedIn Document posts = PDF uploaded as slides. **8-12 slides optimal.** High engagement format: 2-3x more impressions than text-only posts.

> Source: Content pillars from `profile-context.yaml`. Vocabulary from Medical Lexicon DB.

## Universal Slide Structure

| Slide | Purpose | Design Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Slide 1 | Hook title | Bold, large text, question or surprising stat. Must stop the scroll. |
| Slides 2-N | Content | 1 key point per slide. Large readable font. Visual > text-heavy. |
| Final Slide | CTA | "Follow for more" + profile info + engagement question. |

**Design guidelines:**
- Consistent brand colors (professional blue #1B365D / white / accent teal #0097A7)
- Large fonts: title ≥ 36pt, body ≥ 24pt (mobile-first)
- Max 40 words per slide
- Include Medical Lexicon terms where clinically relevant
- Add Hebrew text on relevant slides (bilingual audience)
- Aspect ratio: 4:5 (portrait, optimized for mobile feed)

## 1. Clinical Case Walkthrough (Pillar: Clinical Cases)

**Frequency:** Bi-weekly | **Slides:** 8-10 | **Language:** EN + HE terms

| Slide | Content |
|---|---|
| 1 | **"How I Saved a Tooth Everyone Said to Extract"** (or similar hook) |
| 2 | **Patient Challenge:** Age, chief complaint, referring diagnosis. 1 stat. |
| 3 | **Diagnosis:** Imaging findings (CBCT), complications identified. |
| 4 | **Treatment Plan:** Step-by-step approach overview. |
| 5-7 | **Procedure Steps:** 1 key step per slide. Microscope magnification noted. Instruments named (NiTi rotary files, ultrasonic tips). |
| 8 | **Outcome:** Before/after description. Success metric (e.g., "12-month follow-up: complete periapical healing"). |
| 9 | **Key Takeaway:** 1 clinical lesson in 15 words or less. |
| 10 | **CTA:** "Have you faced a similar case? Share your approach below. Follow for more clinical insights." |

**Hashtags:** #Endodontics #ClinicalCases #SurgicalMicroscope + 1 HE + 1 niche

## Content Bank Integration

When saving carousel content to Content Bank:
- **Type:** "Post"
- **Pillar:** matching pillar name
- **Notes:** "Format: Carousel/Document — [slide count] slides"
- **Language:** "English" or "EN+HE" if bilingual slides included
- **Status:** "Draft" until user creates and publishes the PDF

## Medical Vocabulary Integration

- Use at least 2-3 Medical Lexicon terms per carousel (especially Clinical Verbs and Dental Terms)
- Clinical Case carousels: every procedure slide should use a verified clinical verb
- Tech Explainer carousels: use correct dental terminology (not simplified alternatives)
- Check Avoid flag: never use Avoid-flagged terms on any slide
