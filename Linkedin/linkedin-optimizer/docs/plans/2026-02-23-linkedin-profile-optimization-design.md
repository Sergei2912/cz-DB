```
Open to specialist endodontic positions in private clinics across the Central District and Tel Aviv area. Message me directly or email at the address in my contact info.
```

### Section Rationale

1. **Hook:** Three short punches — volume, quality, instrument. Stops the scroll.
2. **Clinical expertise:** Establishes specialty + names complex case types clinic owners want solved.
3. **IDF paragraph:** Leadership and transferable skills, not just "I was in the army."
4. **Current focus:** Geographic anchoring (3 cities), secondary competencies, bare credentials.
5. **Languages:** Competitive advantage — 1M+ Russian-speaking community signal.
6. **CTA:** Specific target (role, setting, geography), addresses 3 reader types by title.
